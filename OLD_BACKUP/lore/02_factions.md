## 1. Major Factions

### 1.1 The Crystal Syndicate

> *"Crystals flow like blood through the veins of the world. Who controls the blood, controls the heart."*  
> — Founding Doctrine of the Crystal Syndicate

**Type:** Former Merchant Guild (Now Dissolved/Criminal)  
**Founded:** Year -320  
**Headquarters:** Crystal Depths (formerly)  
**Current Status:** Dissolved (Year 892), Remnants Active as Bandit Clans

**History:**

The Crystal Syndicate began as the **Crystal Merchants' Covenant** — a loose alliance of traders who controlled the flow of Essence Crystals from the newly discovered Crystal Depths. For centuries, they operated as legitimate merchants, growing fabulously wealthy.

In Year 847, under the leadership of **Magnus the Faceless** (whose true face was never seen), the Syndicate declared independence from the Kingdom of Valdris. What began as a trade dispute escalated into full-scale war when the Syndicate seized control of multiple crystal mines and refused to pay taxes.

**The Great Gem War** (847-892) ended in the Syndicate's defeat. The Treaty of Five Flames officially dissolved the organization, but legend holds that Magnus escaped with the **Royal Treasury** — a cache of crystals worth more than some kingdoms.

**Modern Day:**

The Syndicate officially no longer exists, but "Syndicate Remnant" bandit clans operate throughout the wilderness. These groups:

- Raid caravans and mining operations
- Control illegal crystal smuggling routes
- Sell their services as mercenaries to the highest bidder
- Search for Magnus's hidden treasury

**Leader:** Unknown (possibly Magnus himself, if he still lives)

**Colors:** Purple and black

---

### 1.2 The Alliance of Five Banners

> *"United we stand, divided we fall. Five flames burn as one."*  
> — Treaty of Five Flames, Year 892

**Type:** Military Alliance  
**Founded:** Year 860  
**Headquarters:** None (Rotating)  
**Current Status:** Active but Fractured

**History:**

When the Crystal Syndicate's power grew too great, five major powers formed the Alliance of Five Banners to oppose them:

1. **The Kingdom of Valdris** — The original challenger to the Syndicate
2. **The Iron Brotherhood** — A warrior culture from the northern mountains
3. **The Maritime Compact** — Alliance of coastal city-states
4. **The Circle of Mages** — The unified magical academy
5. **The Merchant Princes of the Plains** — Southern trading consortium

The Alliance won the Great Gem War, but the peace was uneasy. Each member pursued its own interests, and the alliance gradually fractured. Today, the Five Banners exist in name only — though annual summits are still held to discuss mutual defense.

**Current Status:** Dormant alliance, members act independently

**Symbol:** Five intertwined banners, each with a different color

---

### 1.3 The Kingdom of Valdris

> *"From stone and steel, we build our legacy."*  
> — Royal Motto of Valdris

**Type:** Monarchy  
**Founded:** Year -1,800 (Successor state to Kingdom of Aethor)  
**Capital:** Valdris Keep  
**Current Status:** Major Power

**History:**

Valdris traces its lineage to the noble houses of the fallen Kingdom of Aethor. The first King of Valdris was **Theron the Exile**, a prince who fled west during the First Cataclysm and established a new kingdom in the mountains.

Valdris expanded through military conquest and diplomatic marriage, eventually becoming the dominant power in the northern territories. When the Crystal Syndicate rebelled, Valdris led the resistance — though the war drained its treasury significantly.

**Government:**

- **Monarch:** Queen **Isolde Ironheart** (ruling for 23 years)
- **Council of Lords:** Noble houses that advise the crown
- **The Iron Guard:** Elite military force

**Military Strength:** The largest standing army in the known world

**Economy:** Heavy industry, mining, smithing

**Relations:** Tense with neighboring kingdoms, strong trade with merchant guilds

---

### 1.4 The Circle of Mages

> *"Knowledge is power, but wisdom is survival."*  
> — Motto of the Circle

**Type:** Magical Academy/Organization  
**Founded:** Year -450  
**Headquarters:** The Astral Spire (floating tower in the Crystal Depths)  
**Current Status:** Active

**History:**

The Circle of Mages was founded when the great crystal veins were discovered. A group of scholars and mystics recognized the magical potential of Essence Crystals and established an academy to study their properties.

Over centuries, the Circle developed:

- **Alchemy** — Transmutation of materials
- **Enchantment** — Imbuing objects with magical properties
- **Spirit Binding** — Communication with the Spirit Realm
- **Void Wardcraft** — Protection against corruption

The Circle remained neutral during the Great Gem War, providing magical support to whoever paid them. This pragmatism earned them both wealth and distrust.

**Membership Ranks:**

1. **Initiate** — New students
2. **Adept** — Qualified mages
3. **Master** — Department heads
4. **Archmage** — Council members
5. **Supreme** — The leadership

**Current Leader:** Archmage **Vexillion the Eternal**, who has held the position for 180 years (some say through unnatural means)

**Relations:** Neutral with all, trades with everyone

---

### 1.5 The Iron Brotherhood

> *"Blood tempers steel. Steel tempers the soul."*  
> — Warrior's Oath

**Type:** Warrior Clan/Guild  
**Founded:** Year -600  
**Capital:** Ironhold  
**Current Status:** Active

**History:**

The Iron Brotherhood began as a loose confederation of warrior clans in the northern mountains. They valued strength, honor, and the forge above all else. Over time, they evolved into both a military force and a guild, accepting missions for anyone who could meet their price — provided the mission aligned with their code of honor.

During the Great Gem War, the Brotherhood fought alongside Valdris forces, earning great renown. They specialize in heavy armor, two-handed weapons, and tactical warfare.

**Clan Structure:**

- **The Iron Council** — Twelve clan chiefs
- **Warlord** — Supreme commander (currently **Grimjaw Ironheart**)
- **Bloodsworn** — Elite warriors
- **Aspirants** — Training members

**Specialization:** Heavy combat, siege warfare, monster hunting

**Relations:** Allied with Valdris, hostile to bandits and void creatures

## 2. Guild Organizations

### 2.1 The Adventurer's Covenant

> *"Glory, gold, and the greater good — in that order."*  
> — Common saying among Covenant members

**Type:** Adventurer Guild  
**Founded:** Year 15  
**Headquarters:** Multiple branches in every major city  
**Current Status:** The largest adventurer guild

**History:**

The Adventurer's Covenant was founded by veterans of the Great Gem War who wanted to continue their martial pursuits. Rather than returning to civilian life, they established a guild that could pursue:

- Monster hunting
- Dungeon exploration
- Caravan protection
- Treasure hunting
- Quest completion

The Covenant grew to become the dominant adventurer organization, with branches in every city and town. They maintain a strict code of conduct and a reputation system.

**Guild Structure:**

- **Guildmaster** — Overall leader (currently **Marcus the Golden**)
- **Branch Masters** — City-level leaders
- **Squad Leaders** — Veteran adventurers who lead parties
- **Members** — Rank-and-file adventurers
- **Aspirants** — New recruits on probation

**Services Offered:**

- Quest board (approved contracts)
- Equipment rental and repair
- Skill training
- Team formation
- Insurance against death (for a fee)

**Relations:** Neutral with most, competitive with other adventurer guilds

---

### 2.2 The Solar Vanguard (Historical)

> *"From the light, we rise. In the light, we endure."*  
> — Solar Vanguard Chant

**Type:** Holy Order (Defunct)  
**Founded:** Year -3,000 (Age of First Kings)  
**Headquarters:** The Sun Citadel (now Elm Forest)  
**Current Status:** Dissolved (approximately Year -800)

**History:**

The Solar Vanguard was one of the most prestigious holy orders of the Age of First Kings. Founded by **Brother Solarius**, a priest who claimed to receive visions from the sun itself, the Vanguard grew to become the spiritual guardians of the Kingdom of Aethor.

At their height, the Solar Vanguard:

- Protected pilgrims on sacred routes
- Maintained the Sun Throne's divine right to rule
- Fought against void creatures and dark sorcerers
- Established temples throughout the kingdom

**The Fall:**

The order's fall came gradually. When the First Cataclysm struck, the Vanguard was decimated defending the capital. The survivors retreated to their stronghold in what is now **Elm Forest**, but the order never recovered.

By the Age of Wandering, the Solar Vanguard had dissolved entirely. Their temples fell into ruin, and their secrets were scattered. Some artifacts remain — most notably the **Solar Crown** and **Dawnbringer** — but no one knows who holds them now.

**Legacy:**

The Solar Vanguard is remembered in songs and stories. Some believe fragments of the order still exist, operating in secret, waiting for the time when light is needed again.

**Related Factions:**

- The **Order of the Dawn** claims to be spiritual successors (see below)
- Various "Solar" themed items reference Vanguard artifacts

---

### 2.3 The Order of the Dawn

> *"The sun rises, and with it, hope."*  
> — Order motto

**Type:** Holy Order  
**Founded:** Year 120  
**Headquarters:** The Dawn Citadel (Royal Capital)  
**Current Status:** Active

**History:**

The Order of the Dawn was founded in the Age of Reawakening by priests who sought to restore the spiritual traditions of the old kingdom. While not officially claiming succession from the Solar Vanguard, they embrace similar ideals: protecting the innocent, fighting darkness, and maintaining the sacred flame.

The Order operates:

- Temples in major cities
- Healing services
- Monster hunting (especially undead and void creatures)
- Pilgrimage protection

**Membership:**

- **High Priest/Priestess** — Spiritual leader
- **Templar** — Combat-capable members
- **Acolyte** — Novices
- **LayBrother/Sister** — Non-combat supporters

**Current Leader:** High Priestess **Seraphina Lightbringer**

**Relations:** Allied with Valdris and other kingdoms, hostile to void creatures

---

### 2.4 The Thieves' Shadow

> *"What is locked can be opened. What is hidden can be found."*  
> — Thieves' Code

**Type:** Criminal Syndicate  
**Founded:** Unknown (Pre-Great Gem War)  
**Headquarters:** Hidden throughout the realm  
**Current Status:** Active

**History:**

The Thieves' Shadow is the closest thing the criminal underworld has to an organized guild. They operate in every major city, running:

- Theft services
- Information brokering
- Smuggling
- Contract assassination (denied publicly)

**The Three Tenets:**

1. **Never steal from a brother** — Internal disputes are settled internally
2. **Never kill unless paid** — Murder is a service, not a hobby
3. **The Shadow knows** — Betray the guild, and death follows

**Leadership:** The **Council of Masks** — seven anonymous leaders who wear distinct masks

**Relations:** Enemies of legitimate authority, pragmatic partnerships with Syndicate Remnants

---

### 2.5 The Wardens of the Void

> *"We stand between the darkness and the light."*  
> — Warden oath

**Type:** Specialized Monster Hunter Guild  
**Founded:** Year 200  
**Headquarters:** The Black Watchtower (Void Coast)  
**Current Status:** Active

**History:**

As the Void Rifts began appearing once more, a new guild formed to combat the threat. The Wardens of the Void specialize in:

- Void Rift containment
- Blighted creature extermination
- Spirit realm research
- Anti-corruption wards

They operate primarily in the Black Zones and the Void Coast, but maintain outposts throughout the realm. Their members are marked by **Void-Touched** scars — permanent marks from exposure to Void energy.

**Membership Requirements:**

- Voluntary Void Exposure (grants resistance, scars)
- Specialized training
- Sacred oath binding

**Current Leader:** Warden-Commander **Ashveil**

**Relations:** Allied with Order of the Dawn, monitored with suspicion by other factions

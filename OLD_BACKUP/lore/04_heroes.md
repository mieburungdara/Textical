## 1. Hero Archetypes

### 1.1 The Warrior

> *"Steel and sinew—these never betray."*

**Description:** Warriors are the backbone of any adventuring party. They specialize in direct combat, using weapons and armor to dominate the battlefield. Warriors can tank enemies, deal sustained damage, and control space.

**Common Races:** Human, Orc, Dwarf, Dragonkin

**Playstyle:** Melee combat, high defense, low mobility

**Class Levels:**
- **Novice** → **Warrior** → **Knight** or **Berserker**

---

### 1.2 The Rogue

> *"Strike from the shadows, vanish before they see."*

**Description:** Rogues are masters of stealth, precision, and survival. They strike from unexpected angles, use poisons and traps, and can escape almost any situation. Rogues excel at single-target elimination and information gathering.

**Common Races:** Human, Elf, Halfling, Vampire

**Playstyle:** Stealth, critical hits, evasion

**Class Levels:**
- **Novice** → **Rogue** → **Assassin** or **Shadowblade**

---

### 1.3 The Mage

> *"Fire. Ice. Lightning. I am the storm."*

**Description:** Mages channel the raw power of Essence Crystals to cast devastating spells. They can destroy groups of enemies, provide crowd control, and manipulate the battlefield with magical barriers.

**Common Races:** Human, Elf, Fairy, Highborn

**Playstyle:** Area spells, high damage, low defense

**Class Levels:**
- **Novice** → **Mage** → **Sorcerer** or **Wizard**

---

### 1.4 The Ranger

> *"The wild is my home. The hunt is my purpose."*

**Description:** Rangers are warriors of the wilderness, skilled with ranged weapons and survival techniques. They can hunt specific targets, set traps, and command nature itself to aid them.

**Common Races:** Elf, Halfling, Human, Werewolf

**Playstyle:** Ranged combat, tracking, nature magic

**Class Levels:**
- **Novice** → **Ranger** → **Hunter** or **Beastmaster**

---

### 1.5 The Cleric

> *"The light protects. The light heals. The light judges."*

**Description:** Clerics are divine servants who channel the power of the Pillars to heal allies and smite enemies. They are essential to any party, providing sustain and protective magic.

**Common Races:** Human, Dwarf, Angel, Fairy

**Playstyle:** Healing, buffs, holy damage

**Class Levels:**
- **Novice** → **Cleric** → **Priest** or **Templar**

---

### 1.6 The Alchemist

> *"The difference between poison and medicine is dosage."*

**Description:** Alchemists use their knowledge of herbs and Essence Crystals to create powerful concoctions. They can brew explosive mixtures, create healing elixirs, and enhance their allies with temporary buffs.

**Common Races:** Human, Elf, Slime, Gnome

**Playstyle:** Item-based combat, buffs, utility

**Class Levels:**
- **Novice** → **Alchemist** → **Potion Master** or **Bombardier**

## 2. Major Hero Characters

### 2.1 Seraphina Lightbringer

> *"In darkness, I am the flame."*

**Race:** Angel (Celestial Being)  
**Class:** Cleric → High Priest  
**Element:** Light  
**Affiliation:** Order of the Dawn

**History:**

Seraphina was not born—she emerged from the Undying Flame during the Feast of the Sun's Return, twenty-three years ago. The priests of the Order declared her a miracle: an angelic being drawn to the material plane by the faithful's devotion.

She grew quickly, both in wisdom and power, displaying an innate connection to the Light that puzzled even the most learned Archmages. By her fifteenth year, she had already mastered healing arts that took ordinary clerics decades to learn.

**Current Role:** High Priestess of the Order of the Dawn, spiritual leader of the faithful

**Personality:** Compassionate but firm, Seraphina believes in redemption but tolerates no evil. She sees the best in people but is not naive to their faults.

**Abilities:**

- **Divine Healing** — Channel light to restore wounds
- **Smite** — Call down holy fire on enemies
- **Aura of Protection** — Grant allies resistance to darkness
- **Resurrection** — Raise the fallen (ultimate ability)

**Equipment:** 
- **Solar Crown** — Artifact of the Solar Vanguard (mythic tier)
- **Dawnbringer** — Holy sword of light (mythic tier)
- **Angelic Robes** — Blessed divine armor (mythic tier)

---

### 2.2 Grimjaw Ironheart

> *"Blood tempers steel. Steel tempers the soul."*

**Race:** Orc  
**Class:** Warrior → Warlord  
**Element:** Earth  
**Affiliation:** Iron Brotherhood

**History:**

Grimjaw earned his name at the Battle of the Shattered Peak, where hebit off the arm of a Syndicate champion in single combat. Though he lost his left eye that day, he gained something far more valuable: the respect of the Brotherhood.

Born in the mountain clans, Grimjaw was trained from childhood in the ways of war. He rose through the ranks quickly, earning the title of Bloodsworn at age twenty, and Warlord at thirty-five—the youngest in the Brotherhood's history.

**Current Role:** Warlord of the Iron Brotherhood, supreme military commander

**Personality:** Grimjaw lives by the Brotherhood's code: strength through discipline, honor in battle, loyalty unto death. He is fiercely protective of his warriors and has little patience for cowardice or treachery.

**Abilities:**

- **Battle Cry** — Rally allies, terrify enemies
- **Shield Wall** — Grant massive defense to allies
- **Cleave** — Strike multiple enemies at once
- **Warlord's Command** — Ultimate tactical buff

**Equipment:**
- **Frostbite** — Legendary two-handed axe
- **Iron Plate** — Masterwork armor
- **Warlord's Banner** — Sacred Brotherhood relic

---

### 2.3 Vexillion the Eternal

> *"Mortality is a disease. I found the cure."*

**Race:** Human ( Lich-Transformed)  
**Class:** Mage → Archmage  
**Element:** Dark / Arcane  
**Affiliation:** Circle of Mages

**History:**

Vexillion was born two centuries ago as a promising young mage. His talent was extraordinary—within fifty years, he had risen to Archmage, the highest rank in the Circle. But it was not enough.

Obsessed with extending his lifespan beyond natural limits, Vexillion began researching forbidden arts. He discovered a method to bind his soul to a phylactery, achieving lichdom. When the Council discovered his transformation, he simply... absorbed them into his power.

No one knows how he maintains his position as Archmage. Officially, he "stepped down" from Council. Unofficially, those who ask too many questions tend to disappear.

**Current Role:** Supreme Archmage of the Circle (the title is technically "First Among Equals")

**Personality:** Vexillion speaks in careful, measured tones. He has seen empires rise and fall, and nothing surprises him anymore. He values knowledge above all else and will trade information freely—as long as you have something worth trading.

**Abilities:**

- **Arcane Blast** — Raw magical force
- **Time Stop** — Freeze time briefly
- **Soul Drain** — Steal life force from enemies
- **Phylactery Bond** — Return from death (passive)

**Equipment:**
- **Staff of Ages** — Contains souls of captured mages
- **Robes of the Void** — Dark enchanted armor
- **Ring of Eternity** — Prevents natural aging

---

### 2.4 Magnus the Faceless

> *"The face you see is merely a mask. The truth is in what lies beneath."*

**Race:** Unknown (possibly Human, possibly something else)  
**Class:** Rogue → Assassin Master  
**Element:** Shadow  
**Affiliation:** Former Crystal Syndicate (Wanted)

**History:**

Magnus rose from nothing to lead the Crystal Syndicate at its height. His rise coincided with his adoption of the "Faceless" persona—his true face has never been seen. Some say he wears a mask; others say he has no face at all.

During the Great Gem War, Magnus led the Syndicate forces in a desperate defense of the Crystal Depths. When the Alliance finally breached the final defenses, he vanished—not captured, not killed, simply gone.

**Wanted Status:** Alive, considered extremely dangerous

**Personality:** Magnus is a pragmatist to the extreme. He believes that all systems—political, economic, magical—can be exploited. He has no loyalty to anyone but himself, and no principle he won't abandon if the price is right.

**Abilities:**

- **Shadow Step** — Teleport through shadows
- **Assassinate** — Lethal critical strikes
- **Smoke Bomb** — Escape from any situation
- **The Faceless** — Become completely invisible

**Equipment:**
- **The Faceless Mask** — Identity concealment artifact
- **Shadow Dagger** — Blade that ignores armor
- **Syndicate Signet** — Proof of former leadership

---

### 2.5 Ashveil

> *"The Void is patient. It has already won."*

**Race:** Human (Void-Touched)  
**Class:** Ranger → Warden Commander  
**Element:** Void  
**Affiliation:** Wardens of the Void

**History:**

Ashveil was not born—she was made. She was once an ordinary soldier in the Valdris army, stationed at the Void Coast. During a massive Void Rift eruption, she was exposed to pure Void energy.

Most who experience such exposure die—or worse, transform into Blighted creatures. Ashveil survived, but she was forever changed. Her eyes turned silver, her hair bleached white, and she gained the ability to see Void creatures that others cannot.

She joined the Wardens immediately after her recovery, rising through their ranks with unnatural speed. Some say she makes deals with the very darkness she fights; others say she is the Void's chosen weapon against itself.

**Current Role:** Warden-Commander, leader of all Warden forces

**Personality:** Ashveil speaks rarely and only when necessary. She has seen the darkness that lurks beyond reality, and it has given her a grim perspective on the world. She fights not out of hope, but out of duty—there is no other option.

**Abilities:**

- **Void Sight** — See invisible/void creatures
- **Corruption Shot** — Void-imbued arrows
- **Shadow Meld** — Become one with darkness
- **The Void's Edge** — Blade that harms void creatures

**Equipment:**
- **Warden's Bow** — Crafted from void-touched wood
- **Void Scars** — Permanent marks of power
- **The Black Watch** — Commander's token

---

### 2.6 Zephyr the Wanderer

> *"Home is where the road takes you."*

**Race:** Fairy  
**Class:** Ranger → Beastmaster  
**Element:** Wind  
**Affiliation:** None (Wandering)

**History:**

Zephyr is one of the last of the Winged Folk—a race of fairy-like beings who once dwelt in the great forests. The First Cataclysm destroyed their forest home, and the survivors scattered across the realm.

Zephyr was born in captivity, captured by a collector who hoped to sell her to a noble. She escaped at age twelve, taking with her only her innate magic and a deep distrust of civilization.

Since then, she has wandered the wilderness, learning to communicate with beasts and spirits. She offers her services as a guide, tracker, and companion to those who treat her with respect.

**Current Role:** Wandering tracker, occasional guide

**Personality:** Zephyr is cheerful and curious, but with a wild streak that makes her uncomfortable in cities. She loves adventure, hates being caged, and has a particular soft spot for wounded animals.

**Abilities:**

- **Beast Speech** — Communicate with animals
- **Wind Walk** — Move at incredible speed
- **Summon Beast** — Call animals to aid
- **Nature's Blessing** — Heal animals and nature

**Equipment:**
- **Wind Bow** — Made from fairy-wood
- **Feather Cloak** — Grants limited flight
- **Spirit Whistle** — Calls companion creatures

## % DEX | Tiny5. Progression Systems

### 5.1 Unit Level vs Class Level

**Unit Level (1-100):**
- Represents the hero's physical foundation
- Grants permanent base attribute increases
- Never resets

**Class Level (1-20):**
- Represents professional mastery in current class
- Unlocks skills and abilities
- Resets when promoting to a new class

### 5.2 Promotion

When a hero promotes:
- All attributes receive a permanent +5 bonus
- Class Level resets to 1
- Previous class progress is archived
- New skill tree becomes available

- Resets when promoting to a new class

### 5.2 Promotion

When a hero promotes:
- All attributes receive a permanent +5 bonus
- Class Level resets to 1
- Previous class progress is archived
- New skill tree becomes available

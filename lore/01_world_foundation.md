## 1. The Five Pillars (Simplified)

The world runs on five fundamental forces. Think of them like physics rules that everyone accepts:

- **Kragor** — Raw physical power (STR). Mining, heavy lifting, holding the line.
- **Sylvara** — Processed materials (STR). Woodworking, crafting, turning raw stuff into useful stuff.
- **Noxara** — Getting stuff from monsters (LOOT). Hunting, skinning, harvesting monster parts.
- **Igneth** — Brains and experiments (INT). Alchemy, magic research, figuring things out.
- **Marinor** — Moving precisely (DEX). Fishing, dodging, anything that needs light fingers.

That's it. No gods, no cosmic entities—just the basic rules that make the world work.

---

## 2. Essence Crystals

About 500 years ago, someone found weird glowing rocks underground. These **Essence Crystals** could channel the five forces. Now they're used for:

- Making weapons stronger
- Brewing potions
- Powering magic items
- Pretty much everything in modern society

The crystals are rare and valuable. Whoever controls the mines controls the economy. This led to wars, which brings us to...

---

## 3. The Great Gem War (Year 847-892)

A merchant guild called the **Crystal Syndicate** got too powerful and tried to monopolize all crystal mining. The kingdoms got annoyed, formed an alliance, and kicked their teeth in.

**The Syndicate lost**, but some members escaped with treasure. Now they're just annoying bandits.

---

## 4. Current Era

The world has:
- Several kingdoms fighting for power
- Guilds doing their own thing
- Wilderness areas full of monsters
- Some areas so corrupted they're called "Black Zones"

Adventurers make money by killing monsters, gathering resources, and taking quests. That's the basics.

---

## 5. The Void

There's a dark dimension called the **Void** that occasionally leaks into our world through **Void Rifts**. When this happens, weird shadow monsters appear.

The **Wardens of the Void** are a guild dedicated to dealing with these threats. It's a growing problem but not the main focus of daily life.

## 3. Historical Timeline

### 3.1 The Age of Primordials (Year -10,000 to -5,000)

The era when the Pillars first walked the world in physical form. They shaped mountains, carved seas, and seeded the world with life. This age ended when the Pillars retreated to the **Astral Plane**, leaving their essence to flow through the world.

### 3.2 The Age of First Kings (Year -5,000 to -2,000)

Mortals first united under the banner of the **Kingdom of Aethor**. Seven great kings ruled from the **Sun Throne** in what is now the **Royal Capital**. This was the golden age of civilization—cities rose, magic flourished, and the races lived in relative peace.

The first heroes emerged during this age, learning to harness the power of Essence Crystals. The **Order of the Dawn** was founded to protect the realm from threats beyond mortal ken.

### 3.3 The Age of Wandering (Year -2,000 to -500)

The Kingdom of Aethor fell during **The First Cataclysm** (see below). The surviving peoples scattered across the lands, establishing the independent kingdoms and city-states that exist today. This age is characterized by isolation, tribal conflicts, and the loss of ancient knowledge.

### 3.4 The Age of Reawakening (Year -500 to 0)

The discovery of the **First Crystal Vein** in what would become the **Crystal Depths** sparked a new era. Mages and warriors once again learned to tap into the Pillars' power. The great guilds were founded during this time, and the foundations of the modern world were laid.

### 3.5 The Age of Guilds (Year 0 to Present)

The current era, marked by the rise of powerful adventurer guilds, city-states, and the constant struggle for dominance over Essence Crystal deposits. The **Great Gem War** (see below) reshaped the political landscape, and the world has not known peace since.

---

## 4. The Great Cataclysms

### 4.1 The First Cataclysm — The Sundering (Year -2,156)

The First Cataclysm was not a war, but a cosmic disaster. The **Void Rifts**—tears in reality itself—began appearing across Aethoria, vomiting forth creatures of pure shadow and entropy. The King of Aethor, in his hubris, attempted to seal the rifts using the World Stone fragments.

The attempt failed catastrophically. The **Sun Throne** was destroyed, and the kingdom was sundered into the fractured lands we know today. The **Forbidden Grove** is said to be the site of the final, failed ritual—the epicenter of the corruption that ended the Age of Kings.

### 4.2 The Great Gem War (Year 847-892)

The defining conflict of the current era. The war began when the **Crystal Syndicate**—a merchant guild controlling most Essence Crystal trade—attempted to monopolize all crystal mining in the realm. The great kingdoms and guilds united against them in what became known as the **Alliance of the Five Banners**.

**The War's Timeline:**

- **Year 847:** Crystal Syndicate declares independence from the **Kingdom of Valdris**
- **Year 851:** The Syndicate captures the **Crystal Depths**, establishing their capital there
- **Year 860:** The Five Banners form and begin coordinated resistance
- **Year 875:** Battle of the Shattered Peak — The Syndicate's greatest defeat
- **Year 882:** Fall of the Crystal Depths
- **Year 888:** Last Syndicate stronghold surrenders
- **Year 892:** Treaty of Five Flames signed

**Aftermath:** The Crystal Syndicate was dissolved, but many of its members escaped with vast treasures. The lands they controlled were divided among the victors, but resentment still simmers. To this day, bandit clans and rogue elements claiming to be "Syndicate Remnant" plague the wilderness.

### 4.3 The Second Cataclysm — The Corruption Rising (Year 1,150 — Present)

In recent decades, the Void Rifts have begun appearing once more. The corruption they spread has created the **Black Zones**—regions of utter darkness where normal life cannot survive. The **Forbidden Grove** pulses with renewed power, and scholars fear that the worst is yet to come.

## 5. The Current Era

### 5.1 The World State

The year is approximately **1,256** by the Calendar of Flames. The world is fractured into:

- **Five Major Kingdoms** — Each controlling significant territory
- **The Guild Territories** — Regions controlled by powerful adventurer guilds
- **The Free Cities** — Independent city-states allied with no one
- **The Wilderness** — Unclaimed lands filled with monsters and opportunity
- **The Black Zones** — Corrupted regions where only the desperate or foolish venture

### 5.2 The Crystal Economy

Essence Crystals drive the world's economy. These precious stones are:

- **Mined** from the earth (STR-based gathering)
- **Harvested** from living trees (STR-based gathering)
- **Skinned** from shadow creatures (LOOT-based gathering)
- **Distilled** from magical herbs (INT-based gathering)
- **Fished** from mystical waters (DEX-based gathering)

The crystals are refined into **Crystal Infusions** that empower weapons, armor, and alchemical creations. The control of crystal deposits is the primary source of conflict between nations and guilds.

### 5.3 The Hero's Path

In this age of chaos, heroes rise from all walks of life. The life of an adventurer is dangerous but rewarding:

1. **Novice Phase** — Learning the basics in safe, Green Zones
2. **Journey Phase** — Traveling to Yellow and Orange Zones for better rewards
3. **Champion Phase** — Venturing into Red Zones and dungeons
4. **Legend Phase** — Conquering Black Zones and facing the Void's corruption

## 1. Cosmology

### 1.1 The Origin

In the beginning, there was only the **Void**—an infinite expanse of nothingness, cold and eternal. Within this void, five divine entities known as the **Pillars** slumbered in eternal dream. They were not born, nor created; they simply *were*, existing as fundamental forces that would one day shape reality itself.

The names of these entities, spoken only in ancient rituals:

- **Kragor** — The Mountain's Heart (STR)
- **Sylvara** — The Living Forest (STR)
- **Noxara** — The Shadow's Embrace (LOOT)
- **Igneth** — The Blazing Spirit (INT)
- **Marinor** — The Endless Current (DEX)

### 1.2 The First Dawn

When Kragor stirred from slumber, his first breath became the **World Stone**—a crystalline fragment of divine essence that would become the foundation of all matter. As the other Pillars awakened, they contributed their essence to the stone:

- **Sylvara** breathed life into it, creating the first forests and creatures
- **Noxara** whispered secrets of shadow and death
- **Igneth** forged the fires within the earth's core
- **Marinor** filled the oceans with endless motion

The World Stone shattered in the act of creation, scattering across Aethoria as **Essence Crystals**—the source of all magic and power in the realm. To this day, adventurers seek these crystals, for within them lies the raw power of creation itself.

## 1. Cosmology

### 1.1 The Origin

The world of Aethoria was shaped by five fundamental forces—metaphysical concepts that define how energy and matter behave. Ancient scholars named these forces the **Pillars** to help common folk understand them.

These forces aren't gods or sentient beings—they're simply the rules that govern existence. Mages discovered long ago that by channeling these forces through Essence Crystals, they could perform miraculous feats.

The five Pillars:

- **Kragor** — Physical power and endurance (STR)
- **Sylvara** — Material extraction and crafting (STR)
- **Noxara** — Survival and resource gathering (LOOT)
- **Igneth** — Knowledge and transformation (INT)
- **Marinor** — Agility and precision (DEX)

### 1.2 Essence Crystals

Essence Crystals are rare minerals found deep underground that naturally channel the five forces. They were first discovered in the Crystal Depths roughly 500 years ago, and quickly became the foundation of modern civilization.

Crystals can be used to:
- Enhance weapons and armor
- Power magical devices
- Brew potions and elixirs
- Fuel transportation and construction

In recent decades, the Void Rifts have begun appearing once more. The corruption they spread has created the **Black Zones**—regions of utter darkness where normal life cannot survive. The **Forbidden Grove** pulses with renewed power, and scholars fear that the worst is yet to come.

## 1. Cosmology

### 1.1 The Origin

In the beginning, there was only the **Void**—an infinite expanse of nothingness, cold and eternal. Within this void, five divine entities known as the **Pillars** slumbered in eternal dream. They were not born, nor created; they simply *were*, existing as fundamental forces that would one day shape reality itself.

The names of these entities, spoken only in ancient rituals:

- **Kragor** — The Mountain's Heart (STR)
- **Sylvara** — The Living Forest (STR)
- **Noxara** — The Shadow's Embrace (LOOT)
- **Igneth** — The Blazing Spirit (INT)
- **Marinor** — The Endless Current (DEX)

### 1.2 The First Dawn

When Kragor stirred from slumber, his first breath became the **World Stone**—a crystalline fragment of divine essence that would become the foundation of all matter. As the other Pillars awakened, they contributed their essence to the stone:

- **Sylvara** breathed life into it, creating the first forests and creatures
- **Noxara** whispered secrets of shadow and death
- **Igneth** forged the fires within the earth's core
- **Marinor** filled the oceans with endless motion

The World Stone shattered in the act of creation, scattering across Aethoria as **Essence Crystals**—the source of all magic and power in the realm. To this day, adventurers seek these crystals, for within them lies the raw power of creation itself.

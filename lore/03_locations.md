## 1. Royal Cities

### 1.1 The Royal Capital (Valdoria)

> *"Where the sun once shone from the throne, now we build anew."*

**Region ID:** 1  
**Type:** ROYAL (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** CASTLE  
**Description:** The heart of the restoration movement, Valdoria stands on the foundations of the old Sun Citadel. Though the great throne was lost in the First Cataclysm, the city has risen as a beacon of hope. The current royal palace was rebuilt from the rubble of the ancient structure.

**History:**

- Founded on the site of the original Sun Citadel
- Birthplace of the Order of the Dawn
- Site of the Treaty of Five Flames (Year 892)

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Dawn Citadel** — Headquarters of the Order of the Dawn
- **The Sunken Library** — Partially excavated ruins of the old kingdom's greatest archive
- **The Royal Market** — Largest trading hub in the realm

**Economy:** Banking, magic items, political intrigue

**Guild Facilities:** Full range

---

### 1.2 Ironhold

> *"From iron and blood, we forge our destiny."*

**Region ID:** 2  
**Type:** ROYAL (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** CASTLE  
**Description:** The mountain fortress of the Iron Brotherhood, Ironhold is carved directly into the peaks of the Northern Mountains. The city's halls echo with the sound of hammers and the war chants of the Brotherhood's warriors.

**History:**

- Founded by the first Iron Clan chieftains (Year -1,500)
- Never conquered in recorded history
- Primary military base during the Great Gem War

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Great Forge** — The largest smithy in the known world
- **The Hall of Warriors** — Memorial to fallen Brotherhood members
- **The Mountain Gate** — The only entrance to the city

**Economy:** Weapons manufacturing, military contracts, mining

**Guild Facilities:** Military-focused (weapons, armor, combat training)

---

### 1.3 The Astral Spire

> *"Knowledge transcends mortality."*

**Region ID:** 3  
**Type:** ROYAL (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** DUNGEON  
**Description:** A floating tower that orbits above the Crystal Depths, the Astral Spire is the headquarters of the Circle of Mages. The tower defies gravity through ancient enchantments, and its halls contain the greatest collection of magical knowledge in the realm.

**History:**

- Constructed by the Mages of the First Dawn (Year -400)
- Survived the Great Gem War through magical isolation
- Current residence of Archmage Vexillion

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Infinite Library** — Contains scrolls from before the First Cataclysm
- **The Crystal Chamber** — Where Essence Crystals are studied
- **The Void Ward** — A containment area for dangerous magical artifacts

**Economy:** Magical services, spellcasting, artifact identification

**Guild Facilities:** Magic-focused (alchemy, enchanting, spell training)

---

### 1.4 Port Mariner

> *"The sea provides for those who respect her."*

**Region ID:** 4  
**Type:** ROYAL (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** OCEAN  
**Description:** The jewel of the Maritime Compact, Port Mariner is the largest port city in the known world. Ships from every corner of the globe dock here, bringing goods, adventurers, and secrets from distant lands.

**History:**

- Founded by refugee sailors after the First Cataclysm (Year -1,800)
- Headquarters of the Maritime Compact
- Primary departure point for the Shattered Isles

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Grand Harbor** — Can accommodate 1,000 ships
- **The Tidewell Tavern** — Meeting place for captains and smugglers
- **The Lighthouse of Souls** — A beacon said to guide spirits to rest

**Economy:** Shipping, fishing, trade, maritime services

**Guild Facilities:** Maritime-focused (fishing, sailing, water-based quests)

---

### 1.5 The Dawn Citadel

> *"Light endures. Hope remains."*

**Region ID:** 5  
**Type:** ROYAL (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** FAIRY  
**Description:** The religious center of the Order of the Dawn, the Dawn Citadel is a beautiful complex of temples and gardens built around a pool of eternal light. Pilgrims from across the realm come to seek healing and blessings.

**History:**

- Built on the site where Brother Solarius received his first vision
- Never fell to any enemy in its 1,100-year history
- Houses the relic known as the **Undying Flame**

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Temple of the Sun** — Main religious structure
- **The Garden of Dawn** — Sacred botanical garden
- **The Reliquary** — Contains artifacts from the Solar Vanguard era

**Economy:** Pilgrimage tourism, religious goods, healing services

**Guild Facilities:** Healing-focused (temples, clinics, blessed items)

## 2. Major Towns

### 2.1 Silas' Landing

> *"Where the legendary logger first touched earth."*

**Region ID:** 6  
**Type:** TOWN (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 1  
**Visual Type:** FOREST  
**Description:** Founded by the legendary logger **Silas Elm**, this town serves as the primary logging hub for the realm. The great elm tree from which the town takes its name still stands in the town square, preserved by druidic magic.

**History:**

- Founded by Silas Elm (Year 50)
- Birthplace of the logging industry
- Site of the first guild hall for woodcutters

**Points of Interest:**

- **Silas' Great Elm** — The ancient tree that gave the town its name
- **The Timber Exchange** — Where lumber is bought and sold
- **The Logger's Rest Tavern** — Favorite haunt of woodsmen

**Economy:** Logging, woodworking, herb gathering

**Specialty:** Wood-based crafting, forest quests

---

### 2.2 Copperhollow

> *"Strike the earth, find your fortune."*

**Region ID:** 7  
**Type:** TOWN (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 1  
**Visual Type:** MINE  
**Description:** A bustling mining town at the base of the Iron Peaks, Copperhollow is the primary source of metal ores for the realm. The town's mines extend deep into the mountains, reaching veins of Essence Crystals.

**History:**

- Established during the Age of Reawakening (Year -300)
- Survived multiple bandit raids during the Great Gem War
- Current source of most military-grade metal

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Deep Shaft** — The deepest mine in the realm
- **The Molten Forge** — Public smelting facilities
- **The Miner’s Memorial** — Honors those lost to cave-ins

**Economy:** Mining, smelting, metalworking

**Specialty:** Ore extraction, metal crafting

---

### 2.3 Riverside

> *"The river flows, carrying all things to the sea."*

**Region ID:** 8  
**Type:** TOWN (Safe Zone)  
**Zone Level:** 1  
**Visual Type:** GARDEN  
**Description:** A peaceful agricultural town on the banks of the Great River, Riverside provides food for the realm. Its farms stretch along the river valley, and its fishermen are known throughout the land.

**History:**

- One of the oldest continuously inhabited settlements (Year -800)
- Survived the First Cataclysm through isolation
- Major food supplier during the Great Gem War

**Points of Interest:**

- **The Great River** — Major trade route
- **The Harvest Hall** — Where farmers sell their produce
- **The Water Temple** — Dedicated to Marinor

**Economy:** Agriculture, fishing, river trade

**Specialty:** Food production, fishing quests

---

## 3. Wilderness Regions

### 3.1 Elm Forest

> *"The ancient woods remember the Vanguard's fall."*

**Region ID:** 9  
**Type:** WILDERNESS  
**Zone Level:** 5  
**Visual Type:** FOREST  
**Description:** A vast ancient forest that was once the hunting ground of the Solar Vanguard. Now overgrown and wild, the forest is home to various creatures and the ruins of Vanguard outposts.

**History:**

- Once protected by the Solar Vanguard
- Site of the Vanguard's final stand during the First Cataclysm
- Currently a source of high-quality timber and rare herbs

**Dangers:** Wolf packs, corrupted woodland creatures, bandit camps

**Resources:** Timber (STR), Herbs (INT)

**Notable Features:**

- **The Sunken Temple** — Ruins of a Vanguard shrine
- **The Howling Grove** — Where wolves gather at night

---

### 3.2 Iron Mine

> *"Abandoned during the Great Gem War, now reclaimed by scavengers."*

**Region ID:** 10  
**Type:** WILDERNESS  
**Zone Level:** 8  
**Visual Type:** MINE  
**Description:** An extensive network of tunnels left behind when the Crystal Syndicate was driven out during the Great Gem War. Scavengers and bandits now inhabit the upper levels, while deeper areas are rumored to contain undiscovered crystal veins.

**History:**

- Originally a Syndicate mining operation (Year 850-882)
- Abandoned during the Alliance's final offensive
- Never fully cleared due to collapse risks

**Dangers:** Cave spiders, collapsed tunnels, Syndicate Remnants

**Resources:** Ore (STR), Stone (STR)

**Notable Features:**

- **The Crystal Pockets** — Small crystal deposits in the deeper levels
- **The Collapsed Shaft** — Where the main Syndicate vaults are rumored to be

---

### 3.3 The Crystal Depths

> *"Birthplace of modern magic, now a shadow of its former glory."*

**Region ID:** 11  
**Type:** DUNGEON  
**Zone Level:** 15  
**Visual Type:** DUNGEON  
**Description:** The original discovery site of Essence Crystals, the Crystal Depths were once the capital of the Crystal Syndicate. Now a dangerous dungeon complex, its depths contain both valuable crystals and deadly traps left behind by the Syndicate.

**History:**

- Site of the First Crystal Vein discovery (Year -500)
- Capital of the Crystal Syndicate (Year 851-882)
- Now controlled by the Circle of Mages (surface) and various monsters (depths)

**Dangers:** Syndicate traps, crystal guardians, void creatures

**Resources:** Crystals (all types), Ancient artifacts

**Notable Features:**

- **The Crystal Heart** — The original crystal vein, still pulsing with power
- **The Syndicate Vaults** — Rumored to contain escaped treasure
- **The Mage Outpost** — Surface base of the Circle of Mages

---

### 3.4 Murkfens

> *"Where the light fears to tread."*

**Region ID:** 12  
**Type:** WILDERNESS  
**Zone Level:** 12  
**Visual Type:** SWAMP  
**Description:** A vast swamp filled with toxic mists and dangerous creatures. The Murkfens are said to be cursed, and locals avoid the area unless absolutely necessary.

**History:**

- Site of a failed magical experiment (Year -100)
- The corruption spread, transforming the area into a swamp
- Multiple expeditions have failed to clear the corruption

**Dangers:** Poisonous creatures, toxic mists, bog hags

**Resources:** Hides (LOOT), Herbs (INT), Venoms (LOOT)

**Notable Features:**

- **The Witch's Isle** — Where the original experiment took place
- **The Sunken Village** — Ruins of a settlement that predates the swamp

---

### 3.5 The Amber Waste

> *"Gold and death lie beneath the sands."*

**Region ID:** 13  
**Type:** WILDERNESS  
**Zone Level:** 20  
**Visual Type:** DESERT  
**Description:** A vast desert landscape hiding ancient ruins and valuable mineral deposits. The days are scorching, the nights freezing, and sandstorms can appear without warning.

**History:**

- Once a fertile region before the First Cataclysm
- Contains ruins of the old southern kingdom
- Current source of rare desert crystals

**Dangers:** Sand elementals, heat stroke, sand worms

**Resources:** Ore (STR), Desert herbs (INT)

**Notable Features:**

- **The Sunken Palace** — Ruins of a pre-Cataclysm royal palace
- **The Crystal Oasis** — A hidden source of water and crystals

---

### 3.6 Frostfall Peaks

> *"The mountains remember when the world was young."*

**Region ID:** 14  
**Type:** WILDERNESS  
**Zone Level:** 18  
**Visual Type:** SNOW  
**Description:** The northernmost mountains in the known world, perpetually covered in snow and ice. Ancient glaciers hide caves of ice crystals, and frost giants are said to dwell in the highest peaks.

**History:**

- Home to the Frostborn clan before they were wiped out
- Contains ruins of a First Kings fortress
- Source of the most powerful ice crystals

**Dangers:** Frost giants, ice elementals, avalanches

**Resources:** Ice crystals (DEX), Rare herbs (INT)

**Notable Features:**

- **The Frozen Throne** — An ancient ice formation said to grant visions
- **The Giant's Graveyard** — Where frost giant bones litter the valleys

---

## 4. Dungeons

### 4.1 The Forbidden Grove

> *"The epicentre of the corruption that ended the Age of Kings."*

**Region ID:** 15  
**Type:** DUNGEON  
**Zone Level:** 30  
**Visual Type:** HELL  
**Description:** The site of the failed ritual that ended the Age of Kings, the Forbidden Grove is now a corrupted nightmare. Void energy radiates from the center, creating Black Zone conditions. Only the desperate or foolhardy venture here.

**History:**

- Site of the final ritual to close the first Void Rifts (Year -2,156)
- The ritual's failure caused the First Cataclysm
- The corruption has spread over centuries

**Dangers:** Void creatures, corrupted plants, dimensional hazards

**Resources:** Void Essence, Corruption Crystals, Ancient artifacts

**Notable Features:**

- **The Crater** — Where the ritual was performed
- **The Blighted Tree** — A massive corrupted tree at the center
- **The Rift Gate** — An active Void Rift (unstable)

---

### 4.2 The Shattered Peak

> *"Where the mountains broke and the sky fell."*

**Region ID:** 16  
**Type:** DUNGEON  
**Zone Level:** 25  
**Visual Type:** VOLCANO  
**Description:** A mountain that literally split in half during the Great Gem War, the Shattered Peak contains the ruins of a Syndicate fortress and a network of volcanic tunnels. The battle that destroyed the Syndicate here was the war's turning point.

**History:**

- Site of the Battle of the Shattered Peak (Year 875)
- The Alliance forces defeated the Syndicate's main army
- The mountain was literally split by a magical weapon

**Dangers:** Volcanic hazards, Syndicate ghosts, fire elementals

**Resources:** Fire crystals (INT), Obsidian (STR), Ancient weapons

**Notable Features:**

- **The Split** — The chasm through the mountain
- **The Syndicate Crypts** — Where their dead are interred
- **The Volcano Heart** — A chamber of molten crystal

---

### 4.3 The Sunken Library

> *"Knowledge lost, waiting to be found."*

**Region ID:** 17  
**Type:** DUNGEON  
**Zone Level:** 20  
**Visual Type:** RUINS  
**Description:** Part of the Royal Capital, the Sunken Library was submerged during the First Cataclysm. While partially excavated, vast sections remain underwater, containing texts from before the Cataclysm.

**History:**

- The greatest archive of the Kingdom of Aethor
- Submerged during the First Cataclysm
- Partially excavated over the past centuries

**Dangers:** Water elementals, drowned spirits, structural collapse

**Resources:** Ancient texts, water crystals, lost artifacts

**Notable Features:**

- **The Flooded Chambers** — Still submerged sections
- **The Scholar's Quarter** — Living quarters of ancient mages
- **The Vault of Ages** — Where the most valuable texts were stored

---

## 5. Black Zones

### 5.1 The Void Coast

> *"Where the darkness touches the light."*

**Region ID:** 18  
**Type:** BLACK  
**Zone Level:** 85  
**Visual Type:** STORM  
**Description:** The westernmost shores where the Black Zones meet the sea. The sky is perpetually dark, storms rage constantly, and Void Rifts are most common. The Black Watchtower of the Wardens stands as a lone beacon of light.

**History:**

- One of the first areas affected by the Second Cataclysm
- Site of the Black Watchtower's construction (Year 250)
- The Wardens have held the line for centuries

**Dangers:** Void Rifts, Blighted creatures, corrupted sea monsters

**Resources:** Void Essence, Corruption Crystals, Rare sea materials

**Notable Features:**

- **The Black Watchtower** — Warden headquarters
- **The Void Beach** — Where Void creatures first emerge
- **The Stormspire** — A lighthouse that has never gone dark

---

### 5.2 The Blighted Lands

> *"The world ends here."*

**Region ID:** 19  
**Type:** BLACK  
**Zone Level:** 95  
**Visual Type:** WASTELAND  
**Description:** The most dangerous region in the known world, the Blighted Lands are completely corrupted by Void energy. Nothing survives here except the most powerful heroes, and even they rarely return.

**History:**

- Ground zero of the Second Cataclysm
- Multiple Void Rifts maintain constant corruption
- The Wardens have marked it as a lost cause

**Dangers:** Everything, including things not found elsewhere

**Resources:** Extremely rare Void artifacts

**Notable Features:**

- **The Final Rift** — The largest known Void Rift
- **The Dead City** — Remains of a city that once stood here
- **The Wound** — A tear in reality itself

---

## 6. Special Locations

### 6.1 The Private Island

> *"A sanctuary in a turbulent world."*

**Region ID:** 20  
**Type:** SPECIAL  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** CORAL  
**Description:** A tropical island paradise, mysteriously appearing in waters that were previously empty. Some say it was created by a powerful mage, others claim it is a gift from Marinor himself. Adventurers who discover it are granted use of its facilities.

**History:**

- First appeared on maps approximately Year 800
- No known creator or origin
- Currently managed by a mysterious benefactor

**Features:**

- **Personal housing**
- **Crafting stations**
- **Training grounds**
- **Safe storage**

---

### 6.2 The Battle Arena

> *"Where legends are forged in blood."*

**Region ID:** 21  
**Type:** ARENA  
**Zone Level:** N/A  
**Visual Type:** ARENA  
**Description:** A massive colosseum where adventurers test their skills against each other. The Arena is neutral ground where PvP is permitted, and champions can earn great fame and fortune.

**History:**

- Built during the Age of Reawakening
- Renovated after the Great Gem War
- Current location of the Grand Tournament

**Features:**

- **Player vs Player combat**
- **Spectator betting**
- **Champion titles**
- **Tournament events**

---

### 6.3 The Guild Stronghold

> *"A home for those who unite."*

**Region ID:** 22  
**Type:** GUILD  
**Zone Level:** 0  
**Visual Type:** CASTLE  
**Description:** When a guild achieves sufficient power and wealth, they may claim a Guild Stronghold — a fortress that serves as the guild's headquarters and provides powerful bonuses to members.

**History:**

- A relatively new innovation (past 100 years)
- First Guild Stronghold was built by the Iron Brotherhood
- Now essential for major guild operations

**Features:**

- **Guild storage**
- **Guild upgrades**
- **Territory control**
- **Tax collection**

### Price Fluctuation Mechanics

The market uses a supply/demand-based pricing system that dynamically adjusts material prices based on recent trading activity.

#### Market Equilibrium Price
Each material has a base equilibrium price derived from its rarity and utility:

| Material | Base Price (Silver) | Equilibrium Range |
|----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Ore | 25 | 20-30 |
| Wood | 15 | 12-18 |
| Hide | 15 | 12-18 |
| Herb | 20 | 16-24 |
| Ingot | 80 | 64-96 |
| Plank | 50 | 40-60 |
| Leather | 55 | 44-66 |
| Essence | 65 | 52-78 |

#### Supply/Demand Calculation
Price fluctuation is calculated using the following formula:

```
Current Price = Equilibrium Price × (1 + (Demand - Supply) × 0.1)
```

Where:
- **Supply**: Total quantity of material listed for sale in the last 24 hours
- **Demand**: Total quantity of material purchased in the last 24 hours
- **Price Cap**: ±30% from equilibrium price

#### Price Update Frequency
| Time Frame | Update Frequency |
|------------|------------------|
| Peak Hours (18:00-22:00 UTC+8) | Every 15 minutes |
| Off-Peak Hours | Every 60 minutes |
| Daily Reset | 00:00 UTC+8 (full market data reset) |

|------------------|----------------|---------|

| Sell Order | 3% of transaction value | 1,000 Silver |

| Tax Type | Rate | Distribution |
|----------|------|--------------|

| Luxury Tax | 10% | Applied to transactions over 10,000 Silver |

#### Fee Distribution
```
Total Transaction Value: 100%
- Seller Receives: 92% (after 3% sell fee + 5% market tax)
- Market Fee: 3% (covers platform costs)
- Market Tax: 5% (guild treasury: 3%, server: 2%)
```

### Buy/Sell Order System

The market supports limit orders and market orders for efficient trading.

#### Order Types
| Order Type | Description | Time Limit |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| Limit Buy | Buy at specific price or lower | 24 hours |
| Limit Sell | Sell at specific price or higher | 24 hours |
| Market Buy | Buy at current market price | Immediate |
| Market Sell | Sell at current market price | Immediate |

#### Order Matching Algorithm
- Orders are matched by price (best price first) then time (oldest first)
- Partial fills are supported; remaining quantity stays in order book
- Order cancellation is free before execution

#### Order Book Display
The market interface shows:
- Current market price
- 24-hour price chart
- Order book depth (10 best bid/ask prices)
- Recent trades (last 100 transactions)

### Economic Impact Analysis

#### Market System Impact on Game Economy

##### Positive Impacts
1. **Resource Redistribution**: Efficient allocation of materials between players
2. **Price Discovery**: Transparent pricing based on actual supply/demand
3. **Economic Sink**: Fees and taxes reduce silver inflation
4. **Player Engagement**: Encourages trading, speculation, and market analysis
5. **Guild Economy**: Guild treasury benefits from market taxes

##### Negative Risks
1. **Market Manipulation**: Potential for price gouging by large traders
2. **Economic Volatility**: Rapid price swings during peak demand
3. **Botting Risks**: Automated trading could disrupt market balance
4. **Inflation Pressure**: Large silver influxes from high-volume trading

##### Mitigation Strategies
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Market Manipulation | Implement trade volume limits, price change caps |
| Economic Volatility | Introduce market maker bots to stabilize prices |
| Botting Risks | CAPTCHA verification, trade pattern analysis |
| Inflation Pressure | Adjust tax rates based on market activity |

### Economic Impact Analysis of Storage Expansion System

#### Material Sink Analysis

The storage expansion system creates a significant long-term material and silver sink:

| Expansion Type | Total Materials Required (Max Tier) | Total Silver Cost (Max Tier) | Daily Maintenance Cost | GDD Link |
|----------------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------|----------|
| Bag Expansions | 1,000 Ingots | 200,000 Silver | 0 | [Section 7.4 Inventory Management](./GDD-Textical-RPG.md#74-inventory-management) |
| Warehouse Upgrades | 2,500 Ingots | 500,000 Silver | 100 Silver/day (tax) | [Section 7.4 Inventory Management](./GDD-Textical-RPG.md#74-inventory-management) |
| Guild Bank Upgrades | N/A | N/A | 5% of guild tax revenue | [Section 4.6 Guild System](./GDD-Textical-RPG.md#46-guild-system) |

#### Silver Faucet vs. Sink Balance

```
Estimated Daily Storage-Related Economics:
- Total Player Investment: 500,000-1,000,000 Silver per player
- Daily Silver Sink: 100 Silver/player (warehouse tax)
- Total Server-Wide Sink: 100,000-500,000 Silver/day (for 1,000-5,000 players)
- Ingot Sink: 3,500 Ingots per max-level player
```

#### Market Impact

The storage expansion system will:

1. **Increase Ingot Demand:** Ingots become a critical resource for storage upgrades
2. **Silver Circulation Reduction:** High expansion costs reduce silver inflation
3. **Guild Economy Boost:** Guild tax system creates a sustainable guild funding model
4. **Player Retention:** Storage progression provides long-term goals for end-game players

#### Balance Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|---------------------|
| Early-game storage constraints | Default storage is sufficient for levels 1-9; warehouse unlocks at level 10 |
| Late-game storage overflow | Max stack size capped at 990; warehouse capacity capped at 1,600 slots |
| Ingots becoming too valuable | Alternate ingot sources (daily quests, guild rewards) prevent scarcity |
| Silver hoarding | High expansion costs encourage silver circulation |

#### Long-Term Economic Projections

```
After 6 months (assuming 5,000 active players):
- Total ingots consumed: 8,750,000 (70% of players at Tier 5+)
- Total silver consumed: 2,500,000,000 (50% of players at Tier 4+)
- Guild tax revenue: 125,000 Silver/day (average guild size 50)
- Daily silver sink: 500,000 Silver/day
```

The storage expansion system is carefully balanced to:
- Provide meaningful progression without overwhelming players
- Create sustainable economic sinks that counteract material and silver faucets
- Support guild gameplay and community building
- Scale appropriately with player and guild progression

## Material Balance Notes

### Design Principles

1. **Refining Incentive:** Refined materials are 3× more valuable than raw materials to encourage crafting
2. **Regional Specialization:** Each region specializes in one material type to encourage exploration
3. **Faction Bonuses:** Faction membership provides +15% drop rate for regional materials
4. **Economic Sink:** Enhancement and repair systems create continuous material demand
5. **Progressive Difficulty:** Higher-level enemies drop more valuable materials

### Gathering Efficiency and Yield Calculations

Gathering efficiency is calculated based on skill level, tool quality, and faction bonuses.

#### Yield Formula

```
Total Yield = Base Yield × (1 + Skill Bonus + Tool Bonus + Faction Bonus)
```

#### Efficiency Formula (Energy Cost Reduction)

```
Effective Energy Cost = Base Energy Cost × (1 - Skill Efficiency Bonus - Tool Efficiency Bonus)
```

#### Example Calculation (Level 10 Miner with Steel Pickaxe in Frost Peaks)

```
Base Yield (Iron Deposit): 2-3 Ore
Skill Bonus: +10%
Tool Bonus: +5%
Faction Bonus (Frost Kingdom): +15%
Total Yield Range: (2-3) × 1.3 = 2.6-3.9 → 3-4 Ore (rounded)

Base Energy Cost: 20
Skill Efficiency: +20%
Tool Efficiency: +10%
Effective Energy Cost: 20 × 0.7 = 14
```

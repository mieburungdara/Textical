# Silver Faucet System - Textical RPG

## Overview

Silver faucet system defines all sources of silver income for players in Textical RPG. This document focuses exclusively on how players earn silver through various game activities.

## Silver Sources

### 1. Combat Rewards

#### Humanoid Enemy Drops
**Only humanoid enemies drop silver** - all other enemy types (beast, undead, elemental, construct) drop materials only.

| Enemy Type | Base Silver Drop | Drop Rate | Notes |
|------------|------------------|-----------|-------|
| Bandit | 20-50 Silver | 100% | Common humanoid enemy |
| Knight | 30-60 Silver | 100% | Medium humanoid enemy |
| Mercenary | 25-55 Silver | 100% | Medium humanoid enemy |
| Thief | 15-40 Silver | 100% | Common humanoid enemy |
| **Average per battle** | **32.5 Silver** | **100%** | Based on encounter mix |

**Encounter Rate Analysis:**
- Humanoid enemies: ~30% of total encounters
- Non-humanoid enemies: ~70% of total encounters
- Total battles per player: ~10,000
- Humanoid battles: ~3,000
- Non-humanoid battles: ~7,000

**Combat Silver Income:**
- 3,000 humanoid battles × 32.5 Silver = **97,500 Silver**

## Design Philosophy

### Silver Faucet Goals
1. **Sustainable Economy:** Ensure players always have enough silver for essential activities
2. **Reward Progression:** Silver income scales with player level and activity
3. **Multiple Income Streams:** Provide diverse ways to earn silver
4. **Economic Balance:** Maintain healthy faucet:sink ratio

### Silver Faucet Principles
1. **Humanoid Focus:** Combat is primary silver source, but limited to humanoid enemies
2. **Quest Rewards:** Major silver source through story and side content
3. **Material Economy:** Secondary silver source through crafting system
4. **Activity Bonuses:** Daily/weekly activities provide consistent income

## Future Considerations

### Potential Silver Faucet Additions
1. **Achievement Rewards:** Silver bonuses for completing achievements
2. **Event Rewards:** Special silver rewards during limited-time events
3. **Trading System:** Player-to-player trading with silver fees
4. **Guild Wars:** Silver rewards for guild competition participation

### Silver Faucet Tuning
- **Early Game:** Increase humanoid drop rates for new players
- **Mid Game:** Balance quest rewards with player progression
- **Late Game:** Add silver sinks to prevent inflation
